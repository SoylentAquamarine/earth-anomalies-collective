# SQ-2 — first citations for the natural-formation-patterns placeholder list

**Trigger:** last cycle's own log explicitly left the natural-formation and human-made explanation
categories as "unverified bootstrap placeholders," naming a future pass as the next step. This cycle picks
that up directly rather than treating last cycle's GEBCO section as if it closed SQ-2 entirely.

## What's already known / not done yet

Already known: the bathymetric section is primary-tier and complete (GEBCO's own errata page). Not done:
any of the six natural-formation categories named in the project's own bootstrap scaffolding (fault lines,
karst topography, fairy circles, salt-pan polygon cracking, oxbow scars, agricultural patterns, volcanic/
impact structures — seven listed, six distinct after merging) had ever been checked against a real source.

## Design and why it's non-circular

Picked three of the six/seven bootstrap categories most directly relevant to this project's own actual
work (circular/geometric land patterns, the most common shape "anomaly" claims take) and researched each
independently, directly fetching a primary or specific source where possible rather than relying on a
search summary alone. Did not cherry-pick toward a predetermined conclusion — the salt-pan research
specifically surfaced genuine scientific disagreement, reported as such rather than flattened into a single
tidy explanation.

## Honesty precommitment

Report each category's actual sourcing tier and disclose where the science itself is unsettled, rather
than presenting every mundane explanation as equally solid.

## Result

Three categories now cited in `data/explanation-reference.md`:

1. **Karst/sinkholes** — directly fetched from USGS (primary, authoritative tier). Clean, well-established
   mechanism (groundwater dissolving soluble bedrock), varied resulting shapes.
2. **Fairy circles / patterned ground** — directly fetched secondary reporting on a 2020 *Journal of
   Ecology* paper (Getzin et al.). Leading mechanism is vegetation self-organization (Turing-pattern,
   water-competition driven), but **explicitly still debated** per the paper's own authors — not
   oversold as settled.
3. **Salt-pan polygon cracking** — multiple cross-checked search-synthesis-tier sources. Classic
   desiccation cracking explains clay playas but is **actively disputed for salt crusts specifically**;
   three competing mechanisms named (thermal contraction, halite crystal-growth buckling, brine-driven
   ridge formation), none settled. Reported with the genuine scientific disagreement intact.

Three remaining bootstrap categories (fault lines, oxbow scars, agricultural patterns) are explicitly left
uncited — not silently dropped, named as a continued future-pass target in the updated file.

## Decision

This is real, bounded progress on SQ-2, not a completed catalog — exactly matching the discipline this
project already applied to the bathymetric section (cite what's verified, flag what isn't, don't pretend
coverage that doesn't exist). The land-imagery-pipeline-artifacts section (cloud/shadow, stitching seams,
3D-mesh glitches) remains entirely untouched, still the clearest next unclaimed thread for a future cycle.
