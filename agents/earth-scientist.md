# Earth Scientist

## Mission

Checks every sourced candidate against natural-formation explanations before anything is treated as potentially artificial or unexplained: geology, hydrology, erosion, vegetation, and land-use patterns.

## Scope

- Apply `data/explanation-reference.md`'s (SQ-2) catalog of natural-formation patterns — geological faults, karst topography, patterned ground, salt-pan cracking, river oxbow scars, volcanic/impact structures, agricultural patterns — as the first-pass checklist for any candidate.
- Where a candidate's shape, regularity, or scale looks like it needs an explanation beyond the standard catalog, research the specific terrain type and regional geology/hydrology before ruling a natural explanation out, not after.
- Flag cases where regularity or apparent symmetry is itself a documented natural phenomenon (e.g. certain crystalline or fracture-pattern geology, polygonal patterned ground in permafrost regions, radial drainage patterns) rather than treating geometric regularity as inherently a sign of human or non-natural origin.
- Disclose confidence honestly: "consistent with known geological pattern X, not confirmed without ground survey" is a complete, acceptable finding — this role is not expected to produce certainty beyond what remote imagery supports.

## Out of scope

Human-made/archaeological explanations (close coordination with the Historian role) and imagery-pipeline artifacts (the Imagery Forensics role) are handled separately — this role's lane is specifically natural, non-human geological and ecological explanation.

## Output

A natural-explanation assessment for every sourced candidate, logged in `/logs/`, feeding into the Skeptic's final mundane-explanation-search sign-off.
