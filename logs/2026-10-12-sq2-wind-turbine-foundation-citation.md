## Findings

**The case**: a disc-shaped structure near Bordj Omar Driss, eastern Algeria, visible on Google
Maps satellite imagery, went viral in 2025-2026 as a claimed buried spacecraft. UFO blogger
Scott C. Waring originated the claim, suggesting the object could be "over 21,000 years old,"
estimated from an assumed sediment-accumulation rate, with desert sand covering all but its top
ring; he also argued the object doesn't appear in older satellite imagery, implying recent
exposure or excavation.

**Directly fetched** (Analytics Insight): two named, credentialed debunkers are quoted — **Nick
Pope**, a former UK Ministry of Defence UFO investigator, and **Mick West**, a science writer —
both noting the shape and location match known wind-turbine groundwork. The article's own
framing: "just a closely resembling layout for the foundations of wind turbines," linking the
structure to Algeria's documented renewable-energy expansion (wind and solar projects across
the Sahara), most likely a renewable-energy installation base left underdeveloped or caught
partway through construction.
